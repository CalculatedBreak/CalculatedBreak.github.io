The Pizza Edition - Free Online Games

The Pizza Edition is a free online games site that runs entirely in your browser — no downloads, no installs, and no account to sign up for. That also makes it a solid source of unblocked games for school: everything plays straight from a browser tab, so it works fine on locked-down Chromebooks where you can't install anything. You'll find 295+ games across action, racing, puzzle, sports, idle, and .io multiplayer, from quick reflex runs like Polytrack and Slope to deeper games you'll come back to like BitLife and Monkey Mart. Pick one, press play, and it loads in seconds.

Frequently asked questions

What is The Pizza Edition?

The Pizza Edition is a curated library of free browser games built around one idea: getting you into a game fast. There's nothing to install and no launcher to wait on — every game loads straight into your browser tab on whatever device you're using. New titles are added regularly, and the most-played games rise to the top of the full games list so you can always see what's worth trying.

Can I play these games at school?

That's a big part of why The Pizza Edition exists. Every game runs directly in the browser tab — there's nothing to download, no installer, and no admin rights needed — so if the site loads on your school's network, the games behind it run exactly the same as they do at home. School filters vary, so we can't promise every network lets the site through, but nothing here needs more than a normal browser.

How do I play a game?

Open the Games page and pick something, then click it to launch. It opens right in the tab — give it a few seconds the first time while it loads. Controls vary by game: most use the arrow keys or WASD plus the mouse, and every game's page lists its exact controls and goal. If a game supports fullscreen, the button is usually in the corner of the play window.

Is The Pizza Edition free?

Yes — completely. There's no subscription, no paid unlocks, and no account required. Open a game and play. We keep it that way so anyone can jump in, whether you've got five minutes or a whole afternoon.

Can I play on my phone or tablet?

Most games work on phones and tablets as well as on desktop, so you can play on whatever's nearest. Some titles are built around a keyboard and mouse and feel best on a computer — those usually say so on the game's page — while touch-friendly games run smoothly on mobile with on-screen or tap controls.

Can I play with friends?

Plenty of games support multiplayer. Some are local two-player on a shared keyboard, like Basket Random, Boxing Random, and Getaway Shootout. Others, like Paper.io 2, put you in online matches with players around the world. Each game's page tells you whether it's single-player, local co-op, or online.

Do I need to create an account?

No — you can play everything without signing up. Just keep in mind that with no account, progress in games that save locally is tied to your browser, so clearing your browser data can reset it.

Who makes The Pizza Edition?

It's run by a small team that plays these games too. The aim is a clean, fast, no-friction place to play — without the pop-ups, paywalls, and endless loading screens that clog up a lot of free-game sites.
