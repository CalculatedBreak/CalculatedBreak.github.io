Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy explains how Edusphere Academy (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you visit this browser-game collection, use an account, or play a game. Edusphere Academy is a fictional academy-themed website, not a school or university.

Information we handle

  • Account information: If you create or use an account, your email address and password are sent to our authentication provider, Supabase, to register, sign in, and manage password resets. We receive the account and session information needed to provide sign-in features. We do not ask for your real name or other profile details.
  • Play history: The game player saves the game identifier and title, time played, and most recent play time in your browser’s local storage. This history is kept on that browser and is not uploaded by the site’s play-history feature. Clearing this site’s browser storage removes it.
  • Basic technical information: When you load a page, the hosting provider may process standard request information such as your IP address, browser, device, and requested page to deliver and protect the site.
  • Analytics and advertising information: The site uses Google Analytics and Google advertising services. These providers may use cookies or similar technologies and process information such as page visits, device or browser details, and online identifiers, as described in their own policies and settings.
  • Embedded games and external services: Games may be supplied by third parties. When a game or external service loads, its provider may collect information or use storage under its own privacy policy, independently of this policy.

How information is used and shared

We use information to operate the site and its optional account features, remember play history in your browser, understand site usage, display advertising, protect the service, and respond to requests. We share information with providers that support authentication, hosting, analytics, advertising, and game delivery. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect the site, its users, or others.

Cookies and browser storage

The site’s play-history feature uses local storage, and the player uses session storage to remember whether a sign-in reminder was dismissed for the current browser session. Google Analytics, advertising providers, and game providers may use cookies or similar technologies. You can manage or clear browser storage and cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect some features. Google provides controls through My Ad Center and a Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Retention and your choices

Play history remains in your browser until you clear it. Account information is handled by Supabase for as long as needed to provide account services and meet applicable obligations. To request account or personal-information deletion, or to ask a privacy question, email foundersunset@proton.me. You can choose not to create an account, use browser controls to clear local data, and adjust available third-party advertising and analytics settings.

Children’s privacy

This site is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly ask children under 13 to provide personal information. If you believe a child has provided account information, please contact us at foundersunset@proton.me so we can review the request. Third-party games may have their own age requirements and privacy practices.

Security and international processing

We and our service providers use reasonable measures to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Our providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live, subject to applicable law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site or its practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email foundersunset@proton.me.
