About Edusphere Academy

Questions and answers.

A few details about browsing and using the activity library.

What is Edusphere Academy?

Edusphere Academy is a fictional academy-themed collection of free browser games and activities. It is not a real school, college, or university.

Is Edusphere Academy a real university?

No. Edusphere Academy is a fictional theme for this browser-game collection and does not offer courses, degrees, or accreditation.

Are the games free to play?

Yes. The games are free to play in your browser, with no download needed.

How do I find a game?

Browse the featured carousel, use the random game picker, or search and filter the full collection on the All Games page.
